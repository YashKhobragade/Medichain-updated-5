# User Personas

## Persona 1: Dr. Rajesh Kumar - Quality Assurance Manager at PharmaCorp

### Background
- 15 years in pharmaceutical manufacturing
- Responsible for quality control and regulatory compliance
- Tech-savvy but cautious about new technologies
- Based in Mumbai, manages production for Indian and export markets

### Goals & Motivations
- **Primary**: Protect brand reputation from counterfeit medicines
- **Secondary**: Streamline regulatory reporting and audit processes
- **Personal**: Advance career through successful technology adoption

### Pain Points
- Frequent counterfeit incidents damaging brand trust
- Manual audit trail creation is time-intensive and error-prone
- Difficulty proving authenticity to international distributors
- Regulatory compliance requires extensive documentation

### Technology Comfort Level
- Comfortable with enterprise software
- Limited Web3 experience but willing to learn
- Prefers desktop interfaces for work tasks
- Values security and reliability over cutting-edge features

### User Journey Expectations
1. Simple onboarding with clear security explanations
2. Batch creation workflow integrated with existing processes
3. Automated QR code and Keplo tag generation
4. Real-time visibility into product distribution
5. Easy export of compliance reports

### Success Metrics
- Reduced counterfeit incidents reported
- Faster regulatory audit completion
- Increased distributor confidence in products

## Persona 2: Sarah Chen - Pharmacy Chain Operations Director

### Background
- 8 years managing pharmacy operations across 50+ locations
- Responsible for inventory authenticity and supplier relationships
- Moderate technical skills, focused on operational efficiency
- Based in Singapore, serves diverse customer base

### Goals & Motivations
- **Primary**: Ensure only authentic medicines reach customers
- **Secondary**: Reduce liability and insurance costs
- **Operational**: Streamline supplier verification processes

### Pain Points
- Cannot easily verify medicine authenticity from suppliers
- Fear of unknowingly selling counterfeit medicines
- Time-consuming manual verification processes
- Limited visibility into medicine supply chain history

### Technology Comfort Level
- Comfortable with mobile and web applications
- Uses inventory management systems daily
- Minimal blockchain knowledge but open to learning
- Values speed and simplicity in tools

### User Journey Expectations
1. Quick batch verification before accepting inventory
2. Mobile-friendly interface for on-the-go verification
3. Clear visual indicators of authenticity status
4. Integration with existing inventory systems (future)
5. Audit trail for regulatory compliance

### Success Metrics
- Zero counterfeit medicines in inventory
- Reduced verification time per batch
- Improved customer trust and satisfaction

## Persona 3: Priya Sharma - Health-Conscious Consumer

### Background
- 32-year-old software engineer in Bangalore
- Mother of two young children
- Highly educated and technology-savvy
- Concerned about medicine safety after counterfeit incidents in news

### Goals & Motivations
- **Primary**: Ensure medicines are safe for her family
- **Secondary**: Make informed healthcare decisions
- **Personal**: Feel empowered and in control of family health

### Pain Points
- Cannot verify if medicines from pharmacy are genuine
- Relies entirely on pharmacy reputation
- Worried about counterfeit medicines harming children
- Limited options for independent verification

### Technology Comfort Level
- Very comfortable with mobile apps and QR scanning
- Uses various health and wellness apps
- Familiar with online verification systems
- Values privacy and data security

### User Journey Expectations
1. Simple QR code scanning with smartphone
2. Instant verification results with clear status
3. Detailed medicine history and authenticity proof
4. No need to install additional apps (web-based)
5. Privacy protection - no personal data required

### Success Metrics
- Increased confidence in medicine purchases
- Ability to verify 100% of family medicines
- Peace of mind regarding medicine authenticity

## Cross-Persona Insights

### Common Needs
- **Trust**: All personas need reliable authenticity verification
- **Simplicity**: Complex blockchain concepts must be abstracted away
- **Speed**: Verification must be faster than current manual processes
- **Reliability**: System must work consistently without technical failures

### Divergent Requirements
- **Technical Depth**: Manufacturers need detailed data, consumers need simple results
- **Interface Preferences**: Professionals prefer desktop, consumers prefer mobile
- **Integration Needs**: B2B users need system integration, consumers need standalone solution

### Design Implications
- Multi-interface approach (desktop for professionals, mobile-optimized for consumers)
- Progressive disclosure of information based on user type
- Clear visual hierarchy emphasizing trust and security
- Minimal onboarding friction for consumer use cases

These personas guided our interface design decisions and feature prioritization throughout the development process.
